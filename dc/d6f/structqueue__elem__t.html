union { ... } queue_elem_t

Type-specific queue data (union).

Only one variant is active, determined by the queue type:

  • aggr_parent: scheduled or unscheduled queue with child aggregator queues, only used when aggregators have been configured and set up.
  • aggr: EM_QUEUE_TYPE_AGGR (aggregator child queue)
  • output: EM_QUEUE_TYPE_OUTPUT

q_elem_atomic_group_t queue_elem_t::agrp

Atomic group specific data. Only used if the queue belongs to an atomic group. Cannot be placed in the union below (with the other q_elem_*_t's) since a queue might both belong to an atomic group and have aggregator child queues (i.e. .agrp and .aggr_parent might be simultaneously in use).

uint16_t queue_elem_t::valid_check

Check that contents is an EM queue elem.

EM will verify that the ODP queue context actually points to an EM queue elem and not to something else: queue_elem_t *q_elem = odp_queue_context(odp_queue); if (!q_elem || q_elem->valid_check != QUEUE_ELEM_VALID) EM_ERROR(...); Keep first.
